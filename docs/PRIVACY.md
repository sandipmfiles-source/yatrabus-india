# YatraBus India: Privacy & Data Protection Policy

## 1. Zero-Account Philosophy
Passengers are **never required** to create an account, verify an email address, or provide a mobile phone number to use YatraBus India.
- Search, route discovery, timetable checks, and stop views are 100% anonymous.
- Local user preferences (recent searches, selected language) are stored exclusively in the user's local browser storage (`localStorage`) and can be cleared with one click.

---

## 2. Consent-Based Passenger Tracking
- **No Background Collection Without Consent**: The platform never secretly collects location data in the background.
- **Contextual Permission**: Location permission is requested only after the passenger explicitly confirms: *"Yes, I am travelling on this bus"*.
- **Clear Notice**: The application explains that location observations are aggregated into bus-level positions to help other waiting passengers.
- **Revocable At Any Time**: A prominent, persistent button (*"Stop Sharing Location"*) is visible whenever tracking is active.
- **12-Hour Safety Ceiling**: Every tracking session is created with a hard limit of 12 hours, after which tracking terminates automatically.

---

## 3. Data Minimization & Anonymization
- **No Personal Trajectories**: Raw personal movement histories are never displayed publicly.
- Coordinates received from passengers are matched against route corridors and converted into anonymous vehicle observations.
- Contributor submissions generate non-identifiable reference tokens (`SUB-YYYY-XXXX`).

---

## 4. Hardware Independence
- Does not require dedicated proprietary GPS hardware inside buses.
- Eliminates reliance on surveillance tracking or invasive Bluetooth beacons.
